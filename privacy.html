Privacy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

In short: this website has no cookies, no sign-in and no visitor counting. If you take a free copy on our Discord, we keep your Discord account number next to your code, and nothing else. Each of our games has its own privacy page.

Who we are

Cuz Games is an independent game studio run by Yonatan Ohana, in Israel. This page covers this website (cuzgames.com), the free copies we give out on our Discord, and our Discord, TikTok and Instagram. For anything about your privacy, write to privacy@cuzgames.com.

Each game has its own privacy page

What a game knows about you, and what happens when you buy it, is on that game's own page:

  • Off the Couch: privacy (and its terms).

This website

This website sets no cookies, has no sign-in, shows no ads and doesn't count visitors. We don't know who you are when you look around. Three outside places do see that your browser opened this page:

  • This website is hosted by GitHub, which keeps standard records of visits (such as internet addresses) for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
  • The letters on this page come from Google Fonts. To send them, Google receives your internet address and which browser you use, under Google's privacy policy.
  • The game pictures and videos come from Off the Couch's website (offthecouch.cuzgames.com), which is also ours. Opening them there is covered by its privacy page.

Free copies on our Discord

When you press "Get my free copy" in our Discord, Discord tells us which Discord account pressed it. We keep only:

  • Your Discord account number (a long number Discord gives every account; not your name, email or messages).
  • The code we gave you, and when.

We keep them so each account gets one copy, and so pressing again shows you the same code. The account number also tells when the account was made, which is how we check it's at least 7 days old; we don't ask Discord for anything else. When you use the code, it's linked to your Off the Couch account, as its privacy page explains. All of this is kept on Cloudflare, which runs the program behind the button, under its own privacy policy. We never sell or share it, and never use it for ads.

Our Discord, TikTok and Instagram

When you join our Discord, follow us, comment or send us a message, we see what those apps show us: your name there, your picture, and what you write. We use it only to talk with you and look after our community. Those apps also show us totals, such as how many people watched a video. Discord, TikTok and Instagram collect their own information under their own privacy policies.

Children

This website doesn't ask anyone for anything. Discord, TikTok and Instagram are for people 13 and older (or older where the law says so), and the same goes for our free copies there.

Your rights

You can ask us what we keep about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time. For a free copy, tell us your Discord name and we'll find it. Write to privacy@cuzgames.com.

Changes

We may update this page when things change. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Questions
